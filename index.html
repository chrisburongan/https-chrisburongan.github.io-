A little preparation

Everything we need
to begin.

Prepare these details after confirming your package. Mark unused fields with ❌ and let us know which sections you wish to leave out.

Send photos via WhatsApp or Google Drive. Contact us on Messenger for submission details.

01 · Theme & preferences
  • Selected theme name and link.
  • Name order: groom first or bride first.
  • Muslim or non-Muslim invitation wording.

Your confirmed theme cannot be replaced or changed.

02 · Couple & family details

For both the groom and the bride:

  • Nickname and full name.
  • Father’s and mother’s names.
  • Address.
  • Instagram account.
03 · Ceremony & reception

For each event, provide:

  • Day, date, month, and year.
  • Time and time zone. Specify PHT for Philippine events.
  • Venue name and full address.
  • Map or shared location link.
04 · Photos, video & music
  • 14 photos for the album.
  • 2 individual photos of each partner.
  • 1 couple documentary video, if available.
  • Your requested music.
  • Your love story.
05 · Gifts, livestream & RSVP
  • Digital gifts: up to 2 bank accounts. Provide the bank, account number, and account holder’s name.
  • Physical gifts: delivery address, recipient’s name, and WhatsApp number.
  • Livestream: Instagram or Zoom link, if applicable.
  • RSVP: 1 WhatsApp contact number.

Confirm which features are included in your package. Send personal details privately to our team.
